A playground, not a prediction.

A genuine 3D staggered-grid incompressible solver: semi-Lagrangian velocity advection, bounded viscous diffusion and iterative pressure projection. Obstacles block face fluxes in a three-dimensional voxel mask. STL meshes are centered, normalized and voxelized by triangle-ray parity, not their bounding box.

Air, water and oil use representative SI properties near 20°C, but inlet speed and viscosity are compressed into educational normalized display units. This is not Reynolds-matched or validated engineering CFD. The clock is normalized. Density affects the reported SI Reynolds number, not a buoyancy force.

Limits & numerical model

40 × 24 × 24 cells, approximate free-slip boundaries, 28 pressure Jacobi iterations per step. Fixed inlet/outlet velocity and side walls; residual divergence is displayed rather than hidden. Stair-step geometry, coarse resolution and numerical diffusion do not resolve boundary layers or predict turbulence, drag, lift or pressure loads. Features thinner than a voxel can disappear. Open/non-manifold STL files are flagged when checked; above 60,000 triangles the optional topology check is skipped with a warning. No fixed upload size/triangle cap; browser RAM and GPU limits apply. Full-mesh processing runs in a background worker. Parity cannot guarantee a valid interior. Rotated meshes are resampled; intersecting shells and self-intersections are unsupported.

Water is fully submerged single-phase flow: no free surface, waves, splashes, gravity, cavitation, compressibility or multiphase mixing. Display viscosity = 0.000035 + 0.00032 log₁₀(1 + ν/10⁻⁶); inlet = 0.35 + 0.16U. Reference L changes only the SI metric. Uploads stay in your browser.
